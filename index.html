Protecting Animals across South Africa

The National Council of SPCAs (NSPCA) is a South African organisation that focuses on animal protection and preventing cruelty to animals. We have worked as an organization in South Africa since 1955 to provide information about animal welfare, education and responsible animal ownership.

This website was created to be a quick and simple way to learn more about animal welfare and responsibleanimal care. We encourage you to learn more about animal welfare and to report any misstreatment of or cruelty towards to animals. You will find our contact details on our 'Contact Us' page.
