Animal welfare is about making sure that animals are treated with care, respect and compassion. Animals should have their basic needs met and should be protected from cruelty, neglect and unnecessary suffering.

Animal welfare includes the physical and emotional well-being of animals. Responsible owners should make sure that animals have a safe environment, suitable food, clean water and appropriate care.

Looking after an animal is a responsibility that owners have as one of their top priorities. Owners should provide regular care and make sure that their animals are kept in safe and suitable conditions.

Here are the most important and basic aspects of owning a pet:

  • Provide enough healthy food and clean drinking water.
  • Provide suitable shelter and protection from harsh weather.
  • Give animals enough space to move around safely.
  • Provide appropriate veterinary care when needed.
  • Keep animals in a clean and safe environment.
  • Treat animals with care and kindness.

Animal cruelty can include intentionally harming an animal, neglecting its basic needs such as regularly feeding it and keeping an animal in unsuitable and uncomfortable conditions.

Doing your part by reporting any harm done to animals through these means can help prevent further animal harm or cruelty.

Different animals have different needs. Pets, farm animals and wildlife require different types of food, shelter and care. Learning about the needs of an animal can help people provide better care.

Everyone can help improve animal welfare. People can support their local SPCA, volunteer, donate, educate others about responsible animal care or report suspected animal cruelty.

If you see or suspect animal cruelty, contact your local SPCA and provide as much useful information as possible. Reporting concerns can help the appropriate organisation investigate the situation. Contact Us
