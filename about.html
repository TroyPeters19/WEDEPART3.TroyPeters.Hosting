About Us - Our Mission and Vision

The National Council of SPCAs (NSPCA) was founded in 1955 to help bring together all SPCAs and create more consistent animal welfare standards and legislation in South Africa. Over the years, we have grown into a leading organisation for animal welfare, working to prevent cruelty and promote the humane treatment of animals. The NSPCA became a statutory body under the SPCA Act 169 of 1993 and operates nationally, supporting animal protection through education, training and law enforcement.

Our Mission:

Our mission is to protect animals and to continue improving their welfare through laws, education and public support.

Our Vision:

Our vision is to continue improving animal welfare and preventing animal cruelty so that South Africa is a place where animals are treated with respect, compassion and care.
